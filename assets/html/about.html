About Solvexo

Solvexo is a marketplace app that connects independent sellers with buyers in one place. Whether you're browsing for something new or running your own store, Solvexo is built to make that experience simple and reliable.

Our Mission

We want to make it easy for anyone to start selling online and just as easy for buyers to discover products they'll love — all without the usual friction of setting up a storefront or hunting across dozens of apps.

For Buyers

  • Browse products across categories from multiple independent stores
  • Save favourites to your wishlist and track your orders in one place
  • Subscribe to your favourite stores for member pricing and perks
  • Chat directly with sellers and manage everything from one app

For Sellers

  • Set up your own store and list physical or digital products
  • Manage orders, inventory, and customers from a single dashboard
  • Run your own membership plans, loyalty programs, and coupons
  • Use built-in analytics and point-of-sale tools to run your business
